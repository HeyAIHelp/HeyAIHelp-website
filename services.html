Questions

Common questions, plain answers.

How much does it cost to have AI run for my business?

The monthly service starts at $549 a month for Growth, which is about 7 to 8 hours of work each month. Full Ops is $899 a month for about 14 to 15 hours. If you need more in a busy month, additional hours are $60 an hour.

Do I have to learn Claude or any other AI tool?

No. With Claude, Working for You, I run the AI and you get the finished work to review and use. If you would rather learn to do it yourself, that is exactly what the workshops are for.

What kind of work can you take off my plate?

Business documents like bids, proposals, and policies, process documents, client communication templates, one off drafting, Google Business Profile posts, review requests, social media, and search result updates.

How much are your AI workshops?

Open enrollment workshops are $199 per person for a full day, with up to 20 people. A private workshop for your own team is $2,000 flat for up to 15 people. One on one hourly support is $125 an hour.

Do you only work in Salem?

No. I am based in the Salem, Oregon area and always glad to meet local clients face to face, but I work with businesses all over Oregon and outside it too, in person or remotely.

Do you work with contractors and the trades?

Yes, it is a big part of what I do. I spent 20 years in state government, including assisting the commissioner of the Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries with setting state prevailing wage rates. I help with bids, proposals, prevailing wage documentation, and checking contract deliverables against project specs.
